A few questions

What is it?

A beginner-friendly recorder for jam sessions. Record audio and MIDI with multitrack recording support, loop takes, and bring your own VST3 plug-ins for extra instruments and effects. If you are unfamiliar with VST plugins, plenty are available for free.

What is it not?

Not a fully fledged DAW, not a glorified asset browser, not a mixing/mastering/equalizing master suite. No logins. No subscriptions. No paywall.

Why is it free?

Jamming shouldn’t be behind a login or a paywall. GarageBand was always free for me, and I wanted to pass that on. Free forever, no strings attached.

Can I run it on my computer?

Windows
Windows 10 or 11 (x64), about 115 MB. The installer is unsigned. If SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC,” choose More info, then Run anyway. Updates download in the background and install when you quit or choose “restart to update.”
Mac
Apple Silicon (M1 or later), about 130 MB. The installer is signed and notarized. The app tells you when an update is available so you can install it yourself.
Linux
No prebuilt package. You can build it from source.
